PROPERTY PHOTOGRAPHY

Capture spaces at their best.

  1. Use Natural Lighting
  2. Prepare the Property
  3. Choose the Right Angles
  4. Keep Your Camera Straight
  5. Capture Important Features

1. Use Natural Lighting

Open curtains and blinds before photographing a property. Where possible, use daylight and avoid harsh shadows or dark rooms.

2. Prepare the Property

Make beds, straighten furniture, clear kitchen counters and remove personal clutter. Check that the garden and entrance are tidy.

3. Choose the Right Angles

Photograph from a room corner or doorway to show its layout. Avoid extreme wide-angle distortion that misrepresents the space.

4. Keep Your Camera Straight

Keep vertical lines straight and the camera level. Avoid unnecessary tilting and ensure photographs are sharp.

5. Capture Important Features

Include living spaces, bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, outdoor areas and distinctive property features.

PROPERTY VIDEOS & REELS

Create engaging walkthroughs.

  1. Start With a Strong Opening
  2. Record Smooth Footage
  3. Film Vertically for Reels
  4. Keep Videos Clear and Engaging
  5. End With a Call to Action

1. Start With a Strong Opening

Show the property's most attractive feature in the first few seconds to capture attention.

2. Record Smooth Footage

Walk slowly, hold the camera steadily and avoid sudden turns. Record several short clips instead of one long recording.

3. Film Vertically for Reels

Use a 9:16 vertical format for Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and TikTok.

4. Keep Videos Clear and Engaging

Use a logical sequence, readable captions and clean transitions. Avoid covering important property features with text.

5. End With a Call to Action

Include the agent's name, contact information and an invitation to arrange a viewing.

SOCIAL MEDIA

Grow your professional presence.

  1. Optimise Your Professional Profile
  2. Post Consistently
  3. Use Reels and Stories
  4. Write Engaging Captions
  5. Monitor Your Insights

1. Optimise Your Professional Profile

Use a professional photograph, a clear bio, your service area and up-to-date contact details. Make it easy for clients to reach you.

2. Post Consistently

Plan a manageable posting schedule. Include listings, local market information, client education and property highlights.

3. Use Reels and Stories

Share property previews, behind-the-scenes content, viewing updates and short educational videos where appropriate.

4. Write Engaging Captions

Start with an interesting property benefit, include essential details and finish with a clear call to action.

5. Monitor Your Insights

Review reach, engagement, profile activity and available audience information. Use these insights to improve future posts.

MARKETING RESOURCES

Improve your listing presentation.

  1. Create Strong Property Descriptions
  2. Prepare a Listing Checklist
  3. Promote Rentals Professionally
  4. Maintain Consistent Branding
  5. Plan Your Weekly Marketing

1. Create Strong Property Descriptions

Highlight the property's most important features, location and benefits. Keep descriptions accurate and easy to read.

2. Prepare a Listing Checklist

Confirm the price, property specifications, photographs, listing information and required marketing approvals.

3. Promote Rentals Professionally

Explain the value of professional marketing, quality photographs, tenant screening and the rental services offered.

4. Maintain Consistent Branding

Use approved Jawitz branding, clear typography, accurate contact details and consistent presentation across platforms.

5. Plan Your Weekly Marketing

Prepare content in advance. Combine property listings, educational posts, local insights and agent updates.
