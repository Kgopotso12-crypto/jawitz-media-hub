Booking policies and equipment responsibilities

01 BOOKINGS & AVAILABILITY

All bookings submitted through the Jawitz Media Hub are requests and remain subject to availability and confirmation by the Jawitz Centurion Marketing Department.

Submitting a booking request does not guarantee that the requested equipment, service, date or time will be available.

Agents must provide complete and accurate booking information. The Marketing Department may contact the agent to confirm details or propose an alternative arrangement.

02 INSTA360 X5 EQUIPMENT USE

The Insta360 X5 camera and its supplied accessories must be used responsibly and only for approved purposes.

The agent must inspect the equipment at collection and report any existing damage or missing accessories before taking possession.

The equipment must not be lent, transferred or provided to an unauthorised person without prior approval.

The agent must take reasonable precautions to protect the equipment against damage, loss and theft. The camera must be returned by the agreed date and time.

The camera and all accessories must be returned in the same condition as received, excluding fair wear and tear.

03 DAMAGE, LOSS & THEFT

Any damage, malfunction, loss or theft involving the Insta360 X5 camera or its accessories must be reported to the Jawitz Centurion Marketing Department as soon as reasonably possible.

An agent may be required to pay the reasonable cost of repairing or replacing damaged, lost or stolen equipment, depending on the circumstances of the incident and applicable law.

Financial responsibility will be determined following a fair assessment, taking into account the condition of the equipment when collected, the nature of the incident, negligence, misuse, precautions taken and available supporting evidence.

Normal wear and tear will not, by itself, be treated as damage for which the agent must pay.

In the event of theft, the agent may be requested to provide a police case number and other relevant supporting information.

No repair or replacement charge will be imposed automatically without assessing the circumstances and applicable legal requirements.

04 PHOTOGRAPHY & VIDEO SERVICES

Agents requesting photography, video production, editing or other creative services must provide accurate property and project information.

The agent is responsible for obtaining any necessary permissions from property owners, occupants or other relevant parties before photography or filming takes place.

Properties should be appropriately prepared before a scheduled shoot. Agents must advise the Marketing Department of access restrictions or special requirements.

Editing requirements, expected deliverables and turnaround times must be agreed upon before work begins. Delivery dates may change where information, approvals or materials are outstanding.

05 FEES, PAYMENTS & CANCELLATIONS

Where a service, equipment booking or training session carries a fee, the applicable price and payment arrangements must be communicated to the agent before confirmation.

No payment obligation arises merely from browsing the website or submitting an unconfirmed request.

Agents should notify the Marketing Department as soon as possible if they need to cancel or reschedule a confirmed booking.

Any applicable cancellation charges must be disclosed in advance, reasonable in the circumstances and consistent with applicable law.

Where no fee has been agreed, this section does not create a new equipment hire or training fee.

06 PRIVACY & COPYRIGHT

Booking information and personal details will be processed for legitimate booking administration, communication and service delivery, in accordance with applicable South African data protection law, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Agents must not submit unnecessary confidential information or personal information about clients and property occupants.

Agents must ensure that they have appropriate permission to submit photographs, video footage, music, logos and other creative material.

Content produced or supplied through the Media Hub may only be used in accordance with applicable copyright law, permissions and any written arrangements governing its use.

Agents must follow applicable Jawitz branding and marketing requirements when publishing content.

07 LIABILITY & DISPUTES

Agents are expected to use equipment and services responsibly and to comply with reasonable safety and operating instructions.

Any dispute concerning damage, equipment condition, service delivery or financial responsibility should first be raised with the Jawitz Centurion Marketing Department for review.

Where necessary, the matter may be referred to the appropriate Jawitz management representative.

Nothing in these terms excludes rights, remedies or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable South African law.

Before submitting a booking request, agents will be asked to confirm that they have read and understood the applicable booking terms.
